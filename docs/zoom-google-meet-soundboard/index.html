A soundboard for Zoom and Google Meet

Onion Board plays sounds, music and text-to-speech through your mic, so the people in your Zoom or Google Meet call hear them along with your voice. Call apps try hard to filter out anything that isn't speech, though, so each needs one setting changed. Here's which.

Set up Onion Board

  1. Download OnionBoardSetup.exe and run it.
  2. The short guide asks for your mic and headphones. Click Put my sounds straight into my mic and Yes when Windows asks. No virtual cable needed.
  3. Drag sound files onto the window, right-click a pad to give it a hotkey, and play.

Zoom

Zoom's default noise removal treats music and sound effects as noise and wipes them. In Zoom, go to Settings → Audio, and under Microphone modes pick Original sound for musicians. Keep your normal mic selected. That's it: your sounds come through clearly.

Google Meet

  • In Firefox: it works with your normal mic. If sounds get cut and your Meet has a noise cancellation switch (Settings → Audio), turn it off.
  • In Chrome or Edge: these browsers grab the mic in a way that skips Windows' audio effects, Onion Board's included. Use the cable instead: on Onion Board's Setup tab click Use the virtual cable instead, then in Meet pick CABLE Output as your mic (your voice goes through it too) and turn noise cancellation off if it's there.

What you can play

  • Sound effects and clips, with hotkeys so you don't have to switch windows.
  • Music or a video from another program, from the Apps tab.
  • Typed lines read out loud: see text-to-speech.
