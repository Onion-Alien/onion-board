The thing the others don't do: screen triggers

Give Onion Board a picture from your screen, like a game's "YOU DIED" text, a victory banner or a kill icon, and it plays a sound the moment that picture shows up. Set a sad trombone for every death and never press a key. It watches the game's own window, works behind other windows, and runs entirely on your PC.

Switching over

  1. Install Onion Board. Like Soundpad, it puts your sounds straight into your mic, so there's no virtual cable to install (or pick another output to send to, like Voicemeeter). If Soundpad, Resanance, Soundux or EXP Soundboard is on your PC, it offers to bring your sounds over, with their names, categories and hotkeys. Your old app keeps its own copy.
  2. Coming from somewhere else? Drag your sound files or folder onto the window. Most audio and video formats work. (Voicemod keeps your own sounds in your online account, so add the files you first gave it.)
  3. Nothing to change in Discord or your game: they keep your normal mic. In Discord, set Input Profile to Studio so it doesn't filter your sounds out.
  4. Right-click pads to give them hotkeys (the ones you brought over already have theirs).
