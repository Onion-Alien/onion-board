- First start is now three steps instead of up to six. Your mic and headphones share one
  page, then where your sounds go, then "Make it yours".
- "What will you use Onion Board for?" is gone: you tick the tabs you want (Voice, Radio,
  Apps, Triggers) on the last step instead.
- Discord is now a "Set up Discord when I finish" switch on the last step. Ticked, the
  Discord guide opens after setup and opens Discord on its voice settings for you. It's
  ticked by itself on the virtual cable, where Discord has to pick the new mic.
