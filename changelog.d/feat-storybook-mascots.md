- Bun and Hoot have been redrawn in a softer storybook style: rounder faces, glossy eyes,
  little brows, head tilts, Bun's floppy ear and tail, paws that clasp when he hopes and
  fold when he's cross, and Hoot's open beak when he cheers. Every outfit, act and Easter
  egg is still there.
- A new onion logo: the layers sway like a sound wave. Dark themes show it on a dark
  tile with the layers lit in the theme's colours, light themes on a tile of those
  colours. The app, taskbar, tray and website icons use it.
- Clearer icons: a solid speaker, mic, headphones, eye and gear, and rounder sound
  bars.
