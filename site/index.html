Common questions

What do I set in Discord?

Set Input Profile to Custom, Noise Suppression to None and turn Echo Cancellation off, so it doesn't filter your sounds out (not Studio: on your mic, Studio skips Onion Board). Onion Board warns you if one of them is in the way. Already use Voicemeeter, a mixer or OBS? Pick it under Send my sounds to instead, or add it under Also play on.

Why does Windows warn me?

The app isn't code-signed yet (a certificate costs money every year), so Windows doesn't know it. Click More info → Run anyway (or ⋯ → Keep in Edge / Chrome). Every release is scanned on VirusTotal (the link is at the top).

What needs admin, and why?

Only the one-click "straight into my mic" step. It adds a small audio effect to your mic in Windows' audio engine, backs up your mic's settings first, and puts them back when you switch it off or uninstall.

What goes online?

Only what you ask for (searches, downloads, the radio), an update check, and an anonymous daily count you can switch off. Every request is listed in SECURITY.md, and the app shows them live under Settings → Connection → Network activity.

How do I get rid of it?

Uninstall it like any app in Windows' Settings. Your mic goes back the way it was. Your own sounds stay in %APPDATA%\OnionBoard until you delete them.

Can I read the code?

Yes, all of it. The full source is on GitHub.

A sound in the Community tab is mine. How do I get it taken down?

Email onionboard.contact@proton.me with the sound's name and why it's yours, and it comes down. Anyone can also press Report on a sound in the Community tab.

Is it safe?

Why does Windows warn me?

That's normal for a free app from a small developer: the installer isn't "code-signed", which costs hundreds a year. Click More info → Run anyway (or ⋯ → Keep in Edge / Chrome).

Does it touch my game?

No. It only looks at the game's window, like OBS does: no keys, no clicks, no game memory.

Is it safe with anti-cheat?

We think so, but we can't promise it. Onion Watch copies the picture of your game's window or screen the same way OBS and Discord screen share do. It never loads code into the game, reads its memory, installs a driver or presses a key.

We checked the published rules of Riot Vanguard, Easy Anti-Cheat, BattlEye and FACEIT: none of them bans screen capture. They go after drivers, code loaded into the game, and bots or macros that play for you. We haven't tried it in an anti-cheat game ourselves yet, and an anti-cheat can change its rules at any time.

To stay on the safe side, use it for alerts (a queue pops, a message arrives, a timer runs out), not for triggers that help you play. If an anti-cheat ever complains, please tell us. If a game's window comes out black (some anti-cheats do that), watch the screen the game is on instead.

What leaves my PC?

What it sees on your screen never leaves your PC. The app sends only anonymous daily usage stats (its version, which features are used, crash counts and a random ID; never your name, IP address or device info), and you can switch them off.

Can I read the code?

Yes, all of it. The full source is on GitHub.
