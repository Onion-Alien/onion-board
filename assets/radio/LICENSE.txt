The Radio tab's map files, shipped with Onion Board so opening the maps contacts nobody.

globe.gl.min.js: globe.gl 2.46.2 (https://github.com/vasturiano/globe.gl), which
includes three.js (https://threejs.org). Both MIT, texts below.
earth-blue-marble.jpg, earth-night.jpg: NASA Visible Earth (public domain), from three-globe 2.45.2.
ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson: Natural Earth (public domain), from globe.gl's examples.
